Method note: all findings come from WebSearch snippets only (5 searches, no pages fetched; no official CTM/ONCF/Ryanair page was opened). Treat all figures as indicative. Searched 9 Nov 2026.

## Options Tangier to Agadir (bus, train, car, taxi)

### Takeaway
No direct train exists. CTM bus is the main public option (about 12-16 h, MAD 320-460 per one comparator), which is too slow for a 07:45 departure on 20 Dec unless the family sleeps in Agadir the night before.

### Cited Findings
- Bus: Rome2rio estimates about 13 h and MAD 320-460; CheckMyBus lists CTM trips of roughly 12 h to over 16 h, with departures from night to about 22:00. 12go shows "5h 5m", which the search summary judged implausible for a bus. Comparators disagree; no official CTM timetable or fare was found — [Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Tangier/Agadir), [CheckMyBus](https://www.checkmybus.com/agadir/tangier), [12go](https://12go.asia/en/operator/ctm)
- Train: no direct Tangier-Agadir line. Route is train to Marrakech, then bus. Seat61 lists the Al Boraq to Casa Voyageurs plus a classic train to Marrakech, or a direct overnight train leaving Tangier 23:25 and arriving Marrakech 09:01. Rome2rio gives about 5 h 50 m rail Tangier-Marrakech — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/train-travel-in-morocco.htm), [Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/Train/Tangier/Marrakesh)
- Marrakech to Agadir: no ONCF train; buses roughly hourly, 3 h 30 — [Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/train-travel-in-morocco.htm), [Le Mag Voyage](https://www.lemagvoyage.fr/maroc/se-deplacer-maroc/)
- Rome2rio lists a flight option of about 3 h 30 at MAD 550-2,300 (indicative) — [Rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Tangier/Agadir)

### Inferences
- Train plus bus is about 9-10 h of travel before waiting time (the search tool's own estimate, not sourced). Driving or a private car is likely a similar duration to the bus (not sourced).

### Gaps

## Timing for 07:45 on 20 Dec

### Takeaway
Sleep in Agadir on the night of 19 Dec. Same-day travel is impossible and a night bus of 12 h or more would arrive with no margin.

### Cited Findings
- Ryanair help: airport check-in and document-check desks open at least 2 h before departure and close 40 min before; online check-in closes 2 h before. Reports say the airport cut-off becomes 60 min from 10 Nov 2026, with an airport check-in fee (one source says EUR 55 per passenger) — [Ryanair Help](https://help.ryanair.com/hc/bg/articles/12890590563857-When-does-the-check-in-desk-open-and-close-for-my-flight), [RusTourismNews](https://www.rustourismnews.com/2026/10/21/ryanair-changes-airport-check-in-rules-from-november-10/), [Bladi](https://en.bladi.net/ryanair-doubles-airport-arrival-time-moroccan-flights-imposes-late-fees,114457.html)
- Bladi (2025) reported a doubled arrival time for Moroccan flights and late fees; a travel operator FAQ says printed boarding passes are needed on Morocco flights. Gate-closing times conflict (30 vs 40 min) — [Bladi](https://en.bladi.net/ryanair-doubles-gate-arrival-time-adds-100-late-fee-passengers,114540.html), [First Choice FAQ](https://www.firstchoice.co.uk/holiday/faq/how-do-i-check-in-for-my-ryanair-flight-and-how-do-i-get-my-boarding-pass)

### Inferences
- Plan to be at the airport about 2 h before (about 05:45), so leave the Agadir hotel around 05:00. Check in online and print boarding passes. Buses or a pre-booked taxi at that hour are unverified.

### Gaps
- Whether Bladi's "doubled arrival time" for Morocco applies to AGA was not confirmed; the Ryanair page snippet only shows the generic rule.

## Schengen entry (Tenerife)

### Takeaway
A visa does not guarantee entry. Carry passports, visa stickers and trip proof for each family member.

### Cited Findings
- A Spanish consulate notice says a Schengen visa does not automatically grant entry; officers can refuse if purpose and details of the trip are not proven. Passport validity: 3 months beyond departure, 2 blank pages, issued within 10 years — [Spanish MFA](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Consulados/andorra/en/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/Consular/Visados-Schengen.aspx), [BLS Spain Morocco](https://blsspainmorocco.com/rabat/schengen-tourist.php)
- Children apply with their own visa, signed by a parent — [BLS Spain Morocco](https://blsspainmorocco.com/rabat/schengen-tourist.php)

### Inferences
- Carry for all four: passports and valid visas (the children need their own), return tickets, accommodation bookings, travel insurance, funds proof, and family documents (livret de famille or birth certificates) as a precaution (not sourced).

### Gaps
- Whether the visa's entry count and dates cover 20-27 Dec; Canary-specific rules; Ryanair's own document-check requirements for Morocco-origin flights (not found; verify on Ryanair).
