# BJJ / grappling clubs in Tangier for a 10-year-old, with Tanja Balia (طنجة البالية) in focus

Research method note (Nov 7, 2026): every direct page fetch failed with DNS/allowlist errors. The failed hosts were boa-fightwear.fr, bjj.space, ajptour.com and chronicle.gi; the brief already reported bjjmorocco.com and fitness.ma as unreachable. Firecrawl was not used. Everything below comes from WebSearch result snippets and summaries, so details are second-hand. Treat them as leads to confirm with each club. Google Maps ratings could not be retrieved at all.

## Q1. Which BJJ/grappling academies exist in Tangier, and where are they relative to Tanja Balia?

### Takeaway
Combat Academy Tangier (BJJ + MMA, 12 rue Farazdaq) is the standout. A sponsor says it was founded in 2024 in Tanja Balia, and a hotel listing places it about 310 m from a Tanja Balia aparthotel. It is effectively the neighbourhood club. Three other names surfaced but are thinly documented: UFT United Fighters Tangier (Golf Mujahidine), The Grappling Room, and Tanger/Grappling JJB Fight Team.

### Cited Findings
**Combat Academy Tangier / "Team Combat Academy"**
- Bōa Fightwear's partner page (a sponsor) says the club was founded in 2024 in Tanja Balia and aims to "train the elite of African grappling", starting from "a modest but potential-filled gym". It calls the club the one "revolutionizing Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in Tangier". Instagram: @combat_academy_tangier. — [Bōa Fightwear (EN)](https://boa-fightwear.fr/gb/personalization-club-collection/1091-brazilian-jiu-jitsu-and-grappling-outfit-team-combat-academy.html); [Bōa Fightwear (FR)](https://boa-fightwear.fr/fr/personnalisation-collection-club/1091-tenue-de-jiu-jitsu-bresilien-et-grappling-team-combat-academy.html) (page seen only through search snippets)
- A Trip.com hotel page lists "Tangier Combat Academy - Jiu-Jitsu & MMA" as a landmark about 310 m from Living Appart-Hotel, which is in Tanja Balia. — [Trip.com](https://in.trip.com/hotels/tangier-hotel-detail-123697885/living-appart-hotel/)
- The search summary gives the address as 12 rue Farazdaq, with an English-friendly website and online booking. — [Instagram @combat_academy_tangier](https://www.instagram.com/combat_academy_tangier/); [BJJ Morocco academies](https://bjjmorocco.com/academies) (snippet only)
- TikTok bio, per a snippet: "Jiu-Jitsu Academy MMA 📍 Tangier, Morocco". It mentions a 120 m² mat and adult morning gi/no-gi basics from 07:00 to 09:00. — [TikTok @combatacademytangier](https://www.tiktok.com/@combatacademytangier)

**UFT – United Fighters Tangier**
- A search summary drawn from directory listings (likely bjjmorocco.com) describes a BJJ listing at a Golf Mujahidine address, with French and Arabic as the main languages. Public reviews reportedly mention open-mat training, and the listing advises confirming the current BJJ schedule directly. — [BJJ Morocco academies](https://bjjmorocco.com/academies); [BJJ Morocco map](https://bjjmorocco.com/map) (snippet only)
- The brief gives "golf mujahidine, lot 188" and phone +212 647-464961 from ajptour.com/en/club/13903. I could not re-confirm this because ajptour.com was unreachable, and a search for "United Fighters Tangier" returned no match.

**The Grappling Room**
- Listed on BJJ Morocco's map as a Tangier training venue, with the advice to "contact the academy first". — [BJJ Morocco map](https://bjjmorocco.com/map) (snippet only). No address, coach or schedule was found. The "5.0 from 3 reviews" figure in the brief could not be re-verified.

**Not BJJ, but related: Bushido Ju-Jitsu Academy**
- The Gibraltar Chronicle reports Bushido Ju-Jitsu dojos in Tangier, in the Bendibane area, led by 3rd Dan black belts. This is traditional/Japanese ju-jitsu, not BJJ. The article also mentions children's summer activities, but those apparently refer to the Gibraltar side. — [Gibraltar Chronicle](https://www.chronicle.gi/?p=158985) (snippet only)

### Inferences
- Combat Academy is the clear first choice for a Tanja Balia family. Two independent snippets (the sponsor and Trip.com) put it inside or immediately next to Tanja Balia, so it is walking distance for much of the neighbourhood.
- Golf Mujahidine (UFT) appears to be on the western side of Tangier, in the Mesnana/Golf area. Tanja Balia is on the eastern coastal side, toward Malabata and the Tétouan road. The two are roughly on opposite sides of the city, probably 8–12 km apart by road. This is my own geographic estimate, not sourced; check it on a map.
- Locations of The Grappling Room and Tangier Fight Team are unknown, so I cannot estimate their distance.

### Gaps
- No Google Maps or directory ratings or review themes were retrievable for any club; bjjmorocco.com and fitness.ma were blocked.
- No exact street addresses for The Grappling Room or Tangier Fight Team.
- UFT's address and phone come only from the brief's earlier ajptour lead and were not re-verified.

## Q2. Which clubs run kids classes suitable for a 10-year-old (ages, days, times)?

### Takeaway
Only Combat Academy has documented children's provision. Its TikTok bio, per a search snippet, lists 5 weekly kids classes for ages 6–9 and 5 weekly teen classes for ages 10–14. A 10-year-old would fall into the teen group. Exact days and times were cut off in the snippet.

### Cited Findings
- Combat Academy's TikTok bio lists five weekly kids classes (ages 6–9) and five weekly teen classes (ages 10–14). — [TikTok @combatacademytangier](https://www.tiktok.com/@combatacademytangier) (search snippet)
- According to the brief, the TikTok also mentions the 2025/2026 season, with bookings through combat-academy.sportigo.fr. That platform was not checked in this session.
- No kids-class information was found for UFT, The Grappling Room or Tangier Fight Team. — [BJJ Morocco academies](https://bjjmorocco.com/academies) (snippet)

### Inferences
- With five teen sessions a week, a 10-year-old could probably train two or three times a week. Ask whether a 10-year-old beginner starts in the 6–9 group or the 10–14 group.

### Gaps
- Exact days and times, prices, and whether a trial class is offered: check Instagram, sportigo, or call.
- Whether the season dates stated for 2025/26 have been updated for 2026/27.

## Q6. Alternatives near Tanja Balia: judo, wrestling, ju-jitsu with kids classes

### Takeaway
Searches turned up very little. The only concrete lead is Club Budo Sport, a multisport centre whose activities include judo, karate and tai-jitsu and which has a pool. It is described as near the "Ranbouan Marqala Small Ring Road", which I did not match to a known location. Reviews are mixed. Bushido Ju-Jitsu (Bendibane) is a traditional ju-jitsu option.

### Cited Findings
- Club Budo Sport, Tangier: offers judo, karate, aerobics, tai-jitsu, pilates and yoga, plus a pool. Kids' group swimming costs about 150 MAD and individual lessons about 300 MAD, per a review. Some reviewers praise the cleanliness and equipment; others criticise the management and the lack of experienced staff. — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/12298329/club-budo-sport)
- Bushido Ju-Jitsu Academy has dojos in Bendibane, Tangier, led by 3rd Dan instructors. — [Gibraltar Chronicle](https://www.chronicle.gi/?p=158985)
- French and Arabic searches for judo clubs in or near Tanja Balia returned nothing relevant.

### Gaps
- No wrestling clubs found. No judo club confirmed in Tanja Balia itself. The Royal Moroccan Judo Federation and Wrestling Federation club lists were not consulted.
- Distances from Tanja Balia to Club Budo Sport and to Bendibane are unverified.
