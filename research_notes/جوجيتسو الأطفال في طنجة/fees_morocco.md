# Cost of enrolling a 10-year-old in BJJ (and judo/karate/taekwondo/wrestling) in Tangier and Morocco: published fees in MAD/DH

Research date: 7 November 2026. Method: 12 tool calls. Direct fetches of bjjmorocco.com and decathlon.ma failed because those hosts are blocked in this session's network ("ENOTFOUND"). They would need to be allowed in the session network settings before they can be fetched. Firecrawl was not used. All findings below come from search-engine snippets, which can be summarised or out of context, so treat them as needing confirmation. The search engine is US-centric and returned almost no Moroccan, Arabic-language or Facebook content. A lack of results here does not mean no prices are published on local social media.

Reliability labels: **A** = club or retailer's own page; **B** = directory or aggregator; **C** = search snippet of unclear date, or an unverified figure passed on by someone else.

## Q1. Are there published kids BJJ fees in Tangier (Combat Academy, UFT United Fighters Tangier, The Grappling Room, Tanger Fight Team)?

### Takeaway
No published kids BJJ fee (registration, monthly or package) was found for any Tangier academy. The bjjmorocco.com directory lists the Tangier academies without prices.

### Cited Findings
- The bjjmorocco.com directory lists Combat Academy Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (rue Farazdaq), UFT United Fighters Tangier and The Grappling Room in Tangier. The search snippets show no prices for any of them. Reliability B. — [BJJ Morocco academies](https://bjjmorocco.com/academies); [BJJ Morocco map](https://bjjmorocco.com/map)
- The same site says that when class times or visitor policies are unclear, readers should contact the gym rather than rely on a guess. Reliability B. — [BJJ Morocco](https://bjjmorocco.com/)
- Not a training fee: Tangier grappling-league and "Tanger Open II" tournament entries are reported at €20–€40 per entry. Reliability C, and the snippet does not say whether these are adult or kids entries. — [jiujitsublog.com Morocco tournaments](https://jiujitsublog.com/bjj-tournaments/morocco)
- For general (non-BJJ) context only: HARD GYM Tanger (Ahlan) has a Facebook promotion of "150 DH / mois (de 8h à 14h)". It is for adult fitness, the snippet says the gym is men-only, and the post date is unknown. Reliability C. — [HARD GYM Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/gymcentertanger/)

### Inferences
- In Tangier, kids BJJ prices seem to be given privately by WhatsApp or DM rather than published. Phoning the academies is the only reliable route.

### Gaps
- No registration fee, kids monthly fee, sibling discount or package was found for Combat Academy, UFT, The Grappling Room or Tanger Fight Team.
- I could not reach their Instagram or Facebook posts through search.

## Q2. What do BJJ fees look like elsewhere in Morocco, and are there kids tiers?

### Takeaway
The only Morocco-wide published benchmark is a drop-in of 100–200 MAD from bjjmorocco.com. The site itself warns that most academies do not publish reliable drop-in fees. No kids BJJ tier was found anywhere in Morocco.

### Cited Findings
- "A typical drop-in session runs between 100 and 200 MAD (approximately 10–20 EUR)." This is for adult or visitor drop-ins, date 2026, reliability B. — [How Much Does BJJ Cost in Morocco? (bjjmorocco.com)](https://bjjmorocco.com/articles/how-much-does-bjj-cost-in-morocco)
- The same article says "most city academies in the reviewed dataset do not publish a reliable drop-in fee", so the site does not estimate one. Reliability B. — [bjjmorocco.com cost article](https://bjjmorocco.com/articles/how-much-does-bjj-cost-in-morocco)
- Warriors Fight Academy, Casablanca (adults): 2,299 DH for 3 months, 3,399 DH for 6 months, 5,500 DH per year. I received these figures with the assignment, attributed to bjjmorocco.com, but **could not verify them**. bjjmorocco.com is blocked for direct fetch, and two searches (one for the academy's kids fees, one for the figure itself) returned nothing on the academy or on 2,299 DH. Reliability C. — attributed to [bjjmorocco.com](https://bjjmorocco.com/articles/how-much-does-bjj-cost-in-morocco)
- BJJ surf-camp packages exist (Mat n Wave). These are tourist packages, not local kids enrolment, and the snippet gave no figures. — [matnwave.com/prices](https://matnwave.com/prices)

### Inferences
- If the Warriors figures are right, they work out to roughly 766 DH/month on the 3-month plan and about 458 DH/month on the annual plan for adults (arithmetic only, not a published figure). Kids prices could not be found.

### Gaps
- No kids BJJ tier, registration fee or sibling discount was found for any Moroccan city (Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Agadir).

## Q3. What are kids judo/karate/taekwondo/wrestling monthly fees in Tangier, including associative clubs and دور الشباب?

### Takeaway
No fees were found for Tangier. The only Moroccan kids martial-arts price found is an annual fee at a private sports club in Casablanca: 4,675 DH including insurance. Searches for Darija and Arabic phrasing (شحال الشهرية, واجب الانخراط, دار الشباب) gave nothing relevant.

### Cited Findings
- Campus Sport, Casablanca: judo and karate academies for ages 3–15, **4,675 DH per season, insurance included**, two sessions a week, 1 October to 30 June. The page is about three years old (c. 2023), so the price may be out of date. This is a premium private club, not an association. Reliability B (clubs.ma directory). — [Campus Sport, clubs.ma](https://www.clubs.ma/casablanca/campus-sport)
- CKA Morocco, Casablanca (CIL and Dar Bouazza dojos): karate, judo and other classes for children from age 3. No prices appeared in the search results. Reliability A, but no price. — [CKA Morocco](https://www.ckamorocco.ma/); [CKA Judo Academy](https://www.ckamorocco.ma/all-training/judo-academy/)
- Private martial-arts coaching in Morocco is listed "from 120 DH/h", with a range of 120–500 DH/h. One Casablanca karate coach averages about 300 DH/h. These are private lessons, not club fees. Reliability B. — [ProfesseurParticulier.ma arts martiaux](https://ma.professeurparticulier.com/cours-particuliers/41-arts_martiaux/)
- The Royal Moroccan Judo Federation (FRMJ) runs a judo programme in child-welfare institutions. The snippet names Casablanca first, then Marrakech, Meknes, Rabat, Oujda and Laayoune, not Tangier. It is a social programme, not a paid subscription. — [IJF: launch social program Morocco](https://www--gcp.ijf.org/news/show/launch-social-program-morocco)
- A 2024 Oujda international taekwondo open lists entry requirements (federation affiliation and a sports passport) for younger age categories, but no fee figures. — [martial.events Maroc.pdf](https://www.martial.events/files/events/0/7/8/7/1/Maroc.pdf)

### Inferences
- On a per-month basis the Campus Sport season covers about 9 months, which is roughly 520 DH/month (arithmetic only). It belongs to the upper end of the market and should not be taken as typical of association or دار الشباب fees.

### Gaps
- No published monthly fee or registration fee was found for any kids judo, karate, taekwondo or wrestling club in Tangier, whether association club or دار الشباب.
- No wrestling (المصارعة / lutte) kids fee was found anywhere in Morocco.

## Q5. What do Moroccan federations charge minors for licence and insurance?

### Takeaway
No published licence or insurance fee for minors was found from FRMJ (judo), FRMK (karate), the taekwondo federation, the wrestling federation or any Moroccan BJJ/grappling body. Search results only showed the French federation (FFJDA), which does not apply to Morocco.

### Cited Findings
- The only Moroccan figure that includes insurance is Campus Sport's 4,675 DH season fee "inclut assurance" (Casablanca, c. 2023). The insurance amount is not shown separately. — [clubs.ma Campus Sport](https://www.clubs.ma/casablanca/campus-sport)
- Tournament documents refer to federation affiliation and a sports passport (الجواز الرياضي) as conditions for competing, without costs. — [martial.events Maroc.pdf](https://www.martial.events/files/events/0/7/8/7/1/Maroc.pdf)
- For contrast only, not Moroccan: the French FFJDA licence costs €46 with insurance and €43.80 without accident insurance. — [FFJDA insurance poster 2024-25](https://cfjjb.com/docs/assurance/Affiche_information_FFJDA_2024_2025.pdf)

### Gaps
- No licence or insurance cost for minors from FRMJ, FRMK, the taekwondo federation, the wrestling federation or the Tangier regional leagues (العصب الجهوية). These are usually set each season and shared through the club, so ask the club.
- Sibling discounts: none found for any discipline or city.
