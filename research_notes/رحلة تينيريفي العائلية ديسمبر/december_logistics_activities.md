# Tenerife family trip 20–27 Dec 2026: weather, driving, Christmas closures and family activities (south base, rental car, 2 adults + kids aged 6 and 9)

Research date: 7 Nov 2026. "Verified" = read on an official or primary page. "Reseller" = from a third-party ticket seller (check before relying on it). "Uncertain" = snippet only, or the source is weak.

## December climate (south vs north) and Mount Teide conditions

### Takeaway
AEMET normals show the south (Tenerife Sur airport) is the warm, dry, sunny base in December: mean 19.7 °C, highs about 23 °C, 30 mm of rain over about 3.5 rain days and 195 sunshine hours. The north (Tenerife Norte airport, at altitude near La Laguna) is about 5 °C cooler, with nearly 3x the rain and fog on about 7 days. Snow can close every road up to Teide in late December (it happened on 22 Dec 2024), and the cable car closes on bad-weather days.

### Cited Findings
- **Tenerife Sur Aeropuerto (AEMET station C429I), December normals (verified):** mean 19.7 °C; mean max 22.8 °C; mean min 16.5 °C; rain 30 mm; humidity 66%; 3.5 days with ≥1 mm rain; 0.3 storm days; 0 fog days; 6.3 clear days; 195 hours of sunshine — [AEMET Tenerife Sur](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C429I)
- **Santa Cruz de Tenerife (AEMET C449C, NE coast capital), December (verified):** mean 19.4 °C; max 22.1 °C; min 16.6 °C; rain 43 mm; 5.4 rain days; 3.0 clear days; 168 sunshine hours — [AEMET Santa Cruz](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C449C)
- **Tenerife Norte Aeropuerto (AEMET C447A, Los Rodeos/La Laguna, about 600 m), December (verified):** mean 14.3 °C; max 17.1 °C; min 11.5 °C; rain 82 mm; humidity 79%; 8.8 rain days; 6.6 fog days; 2.6 clear days; 137 sunshine hours. Annual rain is 520 mm and annual fog days 77.7 — [AEMET Tenerife Norte](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C447A&k=coo); [AEMET EN](https://www.aemet.es/en/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C447A)
- **Teide road closures for snow:** on Sunday 22 Dec 2024 the Cabildo de Tenerife temporarily closed the access roads into Teide National Park because of snow and ice on the road. The closed roads were the TF-24 (from La Esperanza), the TF-21 (from La Orotava) and the TF-38 (from Chío) — [Tenerife Más Sostenible / Cabildo](https://tenerifemassostenible.tenerife.es/noticias/buenas-practicas-sostenibles/movilidad/el-cabildo-de-tenerife-cierra-los-accesos-al-teide-por-nieve-y-hielo-en-la-calzada/); [Cabildo de Tenerife Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/CabildoTenerife/videos/-importante-cierre-de-los-accesos-al-teide-%EF%B8%8F%EF%B8%8Fhoy-domingo-22-de-diciembre-de-2024/558134260315354/)
- **Cable car weather closures (verified, official):** the cable car may stop operating in adverse weather. If it cannot run, ticket holders are notified by email and/or SMS at about 8:30 that day. If a weather or technical stop delays boarding by at least 90 minutes past the booked time, a full refund can be requested as long as the tickets have not been used — [Volcano Teide official](https://www.volcanoteide.com/es/volcano_teide/teleferico_del_teide/teleferico_del_teide)
- webtenerife (the official tourism site) says the cable car depends on wind and temperature and can be delayed or closed on bad-weather days. It advises checking real-time status on the official site before setting off — [webtenerife](https://www.webtenerife.com/que-visitar/parque-nacional-del-teide/teleferico-teide/)

### Inferences
- In the south, pack beach and pool clothes plus a light jacket for evenings, which drop to about 16 °C. For Teide (base station about 2,350 m, top station about 3,550 m; altitudes from general knowledge, not checked here) bring warm layers, hats and gloves. Temperatures there can be near or below freezing.
- Rain or cloud in the north (Loro Parque, Puerto de la Cruz, La Laguna) is noticeably more likely than in the south. Keep 1–2 flexible days so a Teide visit can move if roads or the cable car close.

### Gaps
- **Sea temperature:** I found no AEMET or Puertos del Estado figure. December surface water around Tenerife is commonly quoted as about 20–21 °C, but that is unverified here.
- I did not retrieve the Izaña (Teide-area) AEMET December normals.
- I found no statistic on how often the Teide roads close in December. 2024 is one documented example.

## Driving in Tenerife

### Takeaway
The road network is centred on the TF-1 (south and west coast) and the TF-5 (north). I did not verify tolls in this pass; to my knowledge there are none (see Gaps). The Masca road (TF-436) is narrow and winding, and since July 2026 the Cabildo restricts visitor cars there on wildfire or emergency alert days. Canary fuel is cheaper than on the mainland because of a different tax regime, but I could not find a clean December 2026 figure.

### Cited Findings
- **Masca/Teno access rules (July 2026):** on days with an *Alerta* or *Alerta Máxima* for forest fires, or any other emergency declared under the Emergency Plan, access to Masca and Teno is closed to visitor vehicles with purely recreational or tourist purposes. It is also closed to private vehicles not linked to residence, work or essential services. Exempt: residents and owners, workers, public-service maintenance, emergency vehicles, TITSA buses and regular passenger transport, and specially authorised vehicles. The measure is justified by the TF-436's "narrow sections and tight bends where heavy vehicles find it hard to pass" — [Diario de Avisos, 24 Jul 2026](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/07/tenerife-restringira-acceso-masca-y-teno-durante-alertas/)
- The measures were activated from 30 July 2026, between 09:00 and 16:00, "while the risk conditions persist". Bus use (TITSA) rose sharply as a result — [RTVC, 4 Aug 2026](https://rtvc.es/los-controles-de-acceso-a-masca-disparan-el-uso-de-la-guagua-04-agosto-2026/); [Canarias en Pleno (line 355)](https://www.canariasenpleno.com/linea-355-masca-controles-tenerife/)
- **Fuel prices:** the Canary government's energy department publishes fuel prices per island in €/litre — [Gobierno de Canarias – Precios de carburantes](https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/energia/materias/hidrocarburos/carburantes/). Cadena SER reported Canary petrol 98 (E5) at about €1.30/l on 3 Feb 2026 and €1.31 on 23 Feb, before rises in March 2026 — [Cadena SER, 4 Mar 2026](https://cadenaser.com/canarias/2026/03/04/la-gasolina-y-el-diesel-empiezan-a-subir-en-canarias-asi-estan-cambiando-los-precios-radio-club-tenerife/). RTVC reported petrol at €1.716/l in early August 2026, "close to the 2026 record". From the snippet this looks like a national (mainland) average, so it is uncertain — [RTVC, 5 Aug 2026](https://rtvc.es/el-precio-de-la-gasolina-se-acerca-al-record-de-2026-5-agosto-2026/)

### Inferences
- From the south, Masca is reachable via Santiago del Teide. In December a fire alert is unlikely (alerts are typically summer events), but check Cabildo or 112 Canarias notices on the day. With kids aged 6 and 9, expect a slow, twisty drive with possible car sickness and very limited parking in the village.
- Fuel: Canary prices sat roughly €0.30–0.40/l below the mainland in early 2026 (€1.30 vs about €1.6–1.7). Treat the size of the gap as uncertain and check the Gobierno de Canarias page close to travel.

### Gaps
- **Tolls:** I found no official source in this pass confirming TF-1 and TF-5 are toll-free (widely known to have no tolls, but not verified here).
- **Traffic:** I found no source on typical congestion. TF-5 around La Laguna/Santa Cruz and TF-1 around Adeje/Las Américas at rush hour are commonly reported as busy (unverified).
- **Parking:** no rules or blue-zone prices were checked.
- I could not confirm whether the Masca restrictions are still active in November or December 2026.
- I did not find whether the Barranco de Masca trail is open, or its booking system.

## Christmas period: public holidays, closures and events

### Takeaway
On the Canary Islands' official 2026 calendar, 8 Dec and 25 Dec (a Friday in 2026) are public holidays. 24 Dec is not; I found no source on 24 Dec closing hours. The trip window also includes the weekend of 26–27 Dec. Most big attractions stay open; Pirámides de Güímar closes on 25 Dec.

### Cited Findings
- **Canary Islands 2026 regional holidays (verified, Gobierno de Canarias):** 1 Jan, 6 Jan, 2 Apr, 3 Apr, 1 May, 30 May, 15 Aug, 12 Oct, 2 Nov, 8 Dec (Inmaculada Concepción) and 25 Dec (Natividad del Señor). 24 Dec does not appear. Each municipality also sets 2 local holidays — [Gobierno de Canarias news](https://www3.gobiernodecanarias.org/noticias/el-gobierno-aprueba-el-calendario-laboral-de-canarias-para-2026-y-abre-el-plazo-para-fijar-las-fiestas-locales/); Santa Cruz's own list of non-working days — [Sede Santa Cruz](https://sede.santacruzdetenerife.es/sede/la-sede/calendario-dias-inhabiles)
- **Pirámides de Güímar** are "open every day, from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., except the 1st of January and the 25th of December" (verified, official) — [piramidesdeguimar.es](https://www.piramidesdeguimar.es/en/)
- **Teide cable car**, 25 Oct–31 Dec 2026: 09:00–17:00, last ascent 16:00. The official page does not mention a 25 Dec closure — [Volcano Teide tarifas y horarios](https://www.volcanoteide.com/es/teleferico_del_teide/tarifas_y_horarios)
- A Siam Park Facebook post says "we open 365 days a year". It is undated, so treat it as uncertain — [Siam Park Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/siampark/posts/-planea-tu-visita-abrimos-los-365-d%C3%ADas-del-a%C3%B1o%EF%B8%8F-siam-park-tickets-entradas-gener/758729789630067/)

### Inferences
- From general Spanish practice (not verified for 2026): on 24 Dec many shops and supermarkets close early in the afternoon, and on 25 Dec most supermarkets and shops are shut, though many tourist-zone shops and restaurants in Costa Adeje and Las Américas stay open. Stock up on groceries by 23 Dec or the morning of 24 Dec.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 sources on Christmas events (Santa Cruz Christmas market, light displays, nativity scenes/belenes, the Puerto de la Cruz sand nativity). This needs a separate search.
- I found no official 24/25 Dec hours for Loro Parque, Siam Park, Aqualand or Jungle Park, nor for supermarket chains (Mercadona, HiperDino, etc.).

## Family attractions reachable from the south: prices and status

Child ticket bands differ between attractions. Children aged 6 and 9 count as "child" everywhere below, except Aqualand, where the reseller's child band is 5–10.

### Takeaway
Only the Teide cable car prices are verified on an official page: €43 adult and €21.50 child (3–13) return, so about €129 for this family. The other parks' booking engines would not load, so their prices come from resellers. Siam Park is from €44 adult / €32 child (3–11), the Siam + Loro Twin Ticket €78 / €57, and Aqualand €36 adult / €29 child (5–10). Whale-watching boats start at about €27–30 per adult and €13.50–15 per child. Jungle Park appears to still be operating.

### Cited Findings
**Teide cable car / Teide National Park (verified, official)**
- Non-resident return trip: €43.00 adult, €21.50 child. One way: €24.00 adult, €12.00 child. Child = ages 3–13. Hours 25 Oct–31 Dec 2026: 09:00–17:00, last ascent 16:00 — [Volcano Teide tarifas](https://www.volcanoteide.com/es/teleferico_del_teide/tarifas_y_horarios)
- Not allowed to ride: children under 3, pregnant women and people with cardiovascular problems (altitude risk), and people with physical or motor disabilities (evacuation rules). The guided Pico Viejo route is not suitable for under-5s. Free changes or cancellation until 18:00 the day before; after that there are no changes and no refund — [Volcano Teide official](https://www.volcanoteide.com/es/volcano_teide/teleferico_del_teide/teleferico_del_teide)
- Walking to the summit crater (Telesforo Bravo trail) needs a free special permit from the National Park, to be requested well in advance. Tickets sell out days ahead in high season — [webtenerife](https://www.webtenerife.com/que-visitar/parque-nacional-del-teide/teleferico-teide/). ## Entry requirements for Spain/Schengen (general; depends on nationality)

### Takeaway
According to the European Commission, the EES (biometric registration of non-EU travellers at the border) has been fully running in all Schengen countries since 10 April 2026. ETIAS had no confirmed start date as of the latest official wording. The EU says it will announce the date several months before launch, so it is unlikely to apply on 20 Dec 2026. Check this for your nationality.

### Cited Findings
- "The EES is fully operational across all Schengen countries since 10 April 2026. ETIAS is scheduled to start in the last quarter of 2026. The exact start date of ETIAS will be officially communicated later this year." — [European Commission Home Affairs, 28 Apr 2026](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/main-differences-between-ees-and-etias-what-travellers-need-know-2026-04-28_en)
- The official ETIAS site currently says: "The European Union will inform about the specific date for the start of ETIAS several months prior to its launch" — [travel-europe.europa.eu/etias](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias). A third-party site reports that the official site removed its late-2026 target — [etias.com](https://etias.com/articles/etias-drops-2026-date-as-launch-slips-out-of-view)
- EES replaces passport stamping for non-EU nationals on short stays — [travel-europe.europa.eu/ees](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/ees)

### Inferences
- The EES applies to non-EU or visa-exempt travellers. Children are also registered, though I did not verify the rules for under-12s (for example, fingerprint exemptions). Allow extra time at border control on arrival and departure.
- Every child needs their own valid passport (or national ID card for EU citizens). If a child travels with only one parent, a consent letter may be advisable. The Canary Islands are in Schengen, but there are some EU customs differences (outside the EU VAT area), so duty-free allowances apply.

### Gaps
- I did not check Spanish government (interior.gob.es) rules on minors' documents.
- I did not check EES rules for under-12s (fingerprint exemptions).
- I did not confirm whether an ETIAS launch date was announced between Apr and Nov 2026.
- Visa requirements depend on nationality and were not researched.
