# Car rental at Tenerife South Airport (TFS), 20–27 Dec 2026, for 2 adults and 2 children (ages 6 and 9)

Research date: 7 Nov 2026. Each item says whether it is **official** (company, Aena, DGT or BOE), a **live quote**, **third-party/affiliate** or **anecdotal**.

Avis/Budget, Centauro and most others found here are not on it and appear to use off-terminal or shuttle pickup.

## 3. Spanish child restraint law for children aged 6 and 9

### Takeaway
Under Art. 117 of the Reglamento General de Circulación (RD 1428/2003, amended by RD 667/2015), **any minor 135 cm tall or shorter must use an approved child restraint system (SRI) suited to their height and weight, and must sit in the rear seats.** There are narrow exceptions. Height and weight decide the rule, not age. Both children almost certainly need a booster: a booster with backrest (Group II/III, 15–36 kg) is recommended, and the 9-year-old is free of the requirement only if they are already at least 135 cm.

### Cited Findings
- **Official (law text):** "los menores de edad de estatura igual o inferior a 135 centímetros deberán utilizar sistemas de retención infantil" (minors of 135 cm or less must use child restraint systems). "Dichos ocupantes deberán situarse en los asientos traseros" (they must sit in the rear seats). Front-seat exceptions apply only when the car has no rear seats, all rear seats are already taken by such minors, or the SRIs cannot all be fitted in the back. A rear-facing seat may go in the front only if the front airbag is deactivated. — [Art. 117 RGC (Iberley transcription)](https://www.iberley.es/legislacion/articulo-117-reglamento-general-circulacion); the rear-seat priority was introduced by [RD 667/2015, BOE-A-2015-8047](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2015-8047)
- **Official (DGT, page updated 22 Sep 2026):** an SRI matched to weight and height is mandatory up to 135 cm, and as a general rule children always travel in the rear. — [DGT "Con niños"](https://www.dgt.es/muevete-con-seguridad/viaja-seguro/con-ninos/)
- **Official (DGT):** Groups II and III (15–36 kg) are booster seats and booster cushions ("asientos y cojines elevadores"). DGT says that even where a removable-back model is approved for use as a cushion, "es aconsejable su uso siempre con respaldo" (it is always advisable to use it with the backrest). — [DGT "Con niños"](https://www.dgt.es/muevete-con-seguridad/viaja-seguro/con-ninos/)
- **Official (DGT):** under-12s who are 135 cm or taller may use the adult belt directly. DGT recommends an SRI up to 150 cm, and a booster if the belt touches the neck. — [DGT "Con niños"](https://www.dgt.es/muevete-con-seguridad/viaja-seguro/con-ninos/)
- **Official (DGT, 2015):** the rear-seat obligation for minors of 1.35 m or under has applied since October 2015. — [Revista DGT](https://revista.dgt.es/es/reportajes/2015/11NOVIEMBRE/1113ninos-en-el-asiento-de-atras-desde-octubre.shtml)

### Inferences
- The 6-year-old will need a booster, ideally a high-back booster. The 9-year-old needs a booster unless they are at least 135 cm, so measure them before the trip. Both children sit in the back. Only one adult can be in front anyway, so this fits a 5-seat car.
- Not verified on an official source in this research: under UN R129 (i-Size), new booster-cushion approvals are limited to children over about 125 cm. A backless cushion such as Cicar's free booster (see §4) may therefore be less suitable for the 6-year-old. Ask for a high-back booster or bring your own.

### Gaps
- The DGT fine amount for not using an SRI was not fetched in this research (it is commonly cited as €200 plus points; unverified).

## 4. Child seat and booster rental prices

### Takeaway
**Cicar** officially includes child seats and boosters free. **AutoReisen** lends standard baby seats and boosters free, but charges **€5/day, capped at €45 per seat**, for ISOFIX seats. International firms charge per day with a cap. I found no verified TFS-specific figures for them. One anecdotal report puts a seat at about €130 per rental somewhere at TFS.

### Cited Findings
- **Official, Cicar:** "The child-seats, booster-seats and roof racks are free of charge." The child seat is for 0–18 kg, rear seat, forward or rear facing. The booster is for 15–36 kg and "is without backrest, only the seat". The customer fits the seat themselves. — [Cicar conditions](https://www.cicar.com/EN/view/condiciones_en)
- **Official, AutoReisen marketing:** "Baby seats and child boosters are lent without costs", while a luggage rack costs €2/day. — [AutoReisen home](https://www.autoreisen.com/car-hire/car-hire.php)
- **Official, AutoReisen terms:** the ISOFIX baby seat, ISOFIX child seat and ISOFIX booster each cost €5/day, up to a maximum of €45. — [AutoReisen terms (search excerpt; the full-page fetch failed)](https://www.autoreisen.com/car-hire/terms.php)
- **Third-party/affiliate:** neither Cicar nor AutoReisen includes ISOFIX seats free. — [rentcarstenerife.com](https://rentcarstenerife.com/cicar-vs-autoreisen-tenerife/)
- **Official (generic):** Hertz says child seat prices "start from $13.99 per day and vary per location" (this is a US page). Sixt says extras are "charged per day… usually with a daily cap". — [Hertz](https://www.hertz.com/us/en/blog/resources/vas/car-seats-for-children); [Sixt help](https://www.sixt.com/help-center/articles/how-do-i-add-extras-like-a-child-seat-to-an-existing-sixt-booking/)
- **Anecdotal:** "a child seat comes at the cost of 130 euro for the rental" (company not named, Tenerife Facebook group). — [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/747765365412569/posts/2998526557003094/)

### Inferences
- With two children, a local firm saves money. Two ISOFIX boosters at AutoReisen cost at most €70 for 7 days (€35 each), while standard boosters are €0. At internationals, two seats could plausibly cost €100–€260; that range is extrapolated from the anecdote.

### Gaps
- No verified per-day price or cap for child seats at Goldcar, Europcar, Hertz, Sixt, TopCar or Record Go at TFS.
- Child seats are subject to availability: at Christmas, reserve them at booking time.

## 5. Insurance, excess, deposit, cards, fuel, extra driver, age and licence

### Takeaway
The local firms (Cicar, AutoReisen) include **zero-excess comprehensive cover, a free extra driver and unlimited mileage**, and take a low or no deposit. At Goldcar, by contrast, reviewers describe a roughly **€960–€2,300 deposit** or a desk insurance purchase. Non-EU licence holders: DGT advises obtaining an International Driving Permit (IDP) before travel. Licences issued under the Geneva/Vienna convention models, or in Spanish or with an official translation, are valid for visitors.

### Cited Findings
**Cicar (official):**
- Cover is "FULL RISK insurance with no excess". Taxes, airport fees, unlimited mileage, a free additional driver, free young-driver cover (21–25) and out-of-hours service are included. — [Cicar conditions](https://www.cicar.com/EN/view/condiciones_en)
- Fuel works by deposit: "the customer will be asked for a fuel deposit for the amount with which the vehicle is delivered". It is refunded if the car comes back with the same level; otherwise you are charged for fuel actually used. — [Cicar conditions](https://www.cicar.com/EN/view/condiciones_en)
- Minimum age is 21, with at least 2 years of licence. You must carry a printed copy of the contract in the vehicle or risk a fine. There is no fee to cancel or modify. — [Cicar conditions](https://www.cicar.com/EN/view/condiciones_en)
- **Third-party/affiliate:** Cicar holds €200 (standard categories) or €400 (Premium/large MPV, which needs 2 cards). Its written policy is credit card only, though some renters report debit accepted. — [rentcarstenerife.com](https://rentcarstenerife.com/cicar-vs-autoreisen-tenerife/)

**AutoReisen (official):**
- Prices include "no excess insurance", a free extra driver, unlimited mileage and free cancellation. "No debit or credit card will be asked to you during the booking, but just in the moment you pick up your car." — [AutoReisen home](https://www.autoreisen.com/car-hire/car-hire.php)
- An optional "Comfort Plus Super Insurance" covers loss of keys, rims, tyres, wrong fuel and a flat battery. Off-road driving and fines are not covered. — [AutoReisen terms](https://www.autoreisen.com/car-hire/terms.php)
- Fuel policy: "we do not sell petrol, nor do we fill the tank… if the vehicle is returned with more fuel… no payment… will be made". In effect you return the car with the same level you received. — [AutoReisen terms](https://www.autoreisen.com/car-hire/terms.php)
- **Third-party/affiliate:** AutoReisen takes no deposit and accepts debit cards by most accounts. The add-on costs about €2/day, capped near €30. **Anecdotal (Facebook):** "extra 2 euro a day for full insurance cover capped at 30 euro". — [rentcarstenerife.com](https://rentcarstenerife.com/cicar-vs-autoreisen-tenerife/); [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/747765365412569/posts/2545414125647675/)

**Goldcar (DiscoverCars listing, broker terms):**
- Minimum age is 21, with a young-driver fee under 25 and an additional-driver fee for each extra driver. Ferry use needs prior permission and costs extra. — [DiscoverCars Goldcar TFS](https://www.discovercars.com/partners/goldcar-3865)
- **Anecdotal:** reviewers report deposits of "£960 … went up to 1200", "€2300 if no insurance", and "by debit card… 1300 euro, they keep it for 28 days". They also report cards being refused, after which the customer was pushed into desk insurance of €176–€300 a week, and a fuel deposit of about €154 (on TripAdvisor). — [DiscoverCars Goldcar TFS](https://www.discovercars.com/partners/goldcar-3865); [TripAdvisor "Goldcar €1100 deposit"](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g187479-i153-k14671485-o10-Goldcar_car_rental_warning_1100_deposit-Tenerife_Canary_Islands.html)

**Europcar (official, generic):** the deposit is blocked, not debited, on the card at pickup, or within 48 hours before pickup if you use online check-in. — [Europcar deposit policy](https://www.europcar.com/en-us/p/legal-information/deposit-policy)

**Licences (official DGT):**
- EU/EEA licences are fully valid while in date. — [DGT foreign licences](https://www.dgt.es/nuestros-servicios/permisos-de-conducir/permisos-extranjeros-y-de-fuerzas-y-cuerpos-de-seguridad/conducir-con-un-permiso-extranjero/)
- For non-EU/EEA licences, DGT advises "siempre que, antes de venir, obtengas en tu país el permiso internacional" (always obtain the international permit in your home country before coming). Non-EU licences are also valid if they are written in Spanish, carry an official translation, or follow Annex 9 of the Geneva Convention or Annex 6 of the Vienna Convention. Further conditions: the licence is in date, the holder meets the Spanish minimum age, and no more than 6 months have passed since taking up residence in Spain. — [DGT foreign licences (page dated 13/11/2020)](https://www.dgt.es/nuestros-servicios/permisos-de-conducir/permisos-extranjeros-y-de-fuerzas-y-cuerpos-de-seguridad/conducir-con-un-permiso-extranjero/)
- An IDP is valid for 1 year. It is complementary and must be carried with the passport and the national licence. The IDP is printed in several languages, Arabic included. — same DGT page

### Inferences
- For Moroccan or Algerian licence holders (often in Arabic/French), the safest route is to **get an IDP in the home country before travelling**, and to check that the rental company accepts it. Both Morocco and Algeria are Vienna Convention parties (unverified here), but rental desks often require an IDP regardless.
- Cicar requires 2 years of licence and age 21 or over. Both adults should meet this before being added as drivers.
- Bring a **credit card in the main driver's name** even for local firms. AutoReisen is the most debit-friendly option.

### Gaps
- Whether Spain specifically recognises Moroccan or Algerian licences without an IDP for tourists was not confirmed on an official source.
- The rental companies' own IDP rules (Cicar, AutoReisen) were not found in the pages fetched.
- Hertz, Europcar, Sixt, TopCar and Record Go deposits, excess amounts and fuel policies at TFS were not verified.

## 6. Booking timing and common pitfalls

### Takeaway
Book early and directly with a local zero-excess company, and reserve the child seats at the same time. The most common complaints cluster around budget international firms or brokers (mainly Goldcar via brokers): **pressure to buy desk insurance, broker "full cover" not being recognised, large deposits, credit cards refused, fuel penalties, and long queues** (40 minutes to 2.5 hours).

### Cited Findings
- **Anecdotal, Goldcar TFS reviews:** queues of 40 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours and "2 and a half hours" are reported. Goldcar did not accept broker top insurance and charged "£1100 to my credit card". Desk insurance was pushed at about €180–€266 a week even when the customer already had full coverage. One renter was charged "€61 for not returning the car with full fuel… €50 as a penalty and €11 for fuel". Another left a Goldcar booking and went with AutoReisen, "saving over €150". — [DiscoverCars Goldcar TFS](https://www.discovercars.com/partners/goldcar-3865)
- **Anecdotal:** Cicar, AutoReisen, TopCar and Sixt are named in a Tenerife Facebook group as "most reliable… known for transparent rentals". — [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/747765365412569/posts/2926860714169679/)
- **Third-party/affiliate:** Cicar and AutoReisen "consistently outperform the big international brands… on hidden excess, upsell pressure, and fuel disputes." A flat tyre with no spare wheel is cited as a common Tenerife issue. — [rentcarstenerife.com](https://rentcarstenerife.com/cicar-vs-autoreisen-tenerife/)
- **Official:** Cicar has no charge to cancel or modify, and AutoReisen offers free cancellation. Booking early therefore carries little risk. — [Cicar conditions](https://www.cicar.com/EN/view/condiciones_en); [AutoReisen home](https://www.autoreisen.com/car-hire/car-hire.php)
- **Third-party:** Teide and the mountain roads are steep and winding, so choose a car with enough power for 4 people and luggage. Photograph the car and the fuel gauge at pickup, and save the exact return point. — [MySpanishCar](https://myspanishcar.com/tenerife-south-airport-car-rental.html)

### Inferences
- Because local firms allow free cancellation, the best approach is to **book now** (7 weeks out) with AutoReisen or Cicar. Note the live Christmas prices run about 25–40% above January's. Re-check closer to the date and rebook if the price falls.
- If you book through a broker, its "full cover" is reimbursement insurance. The rental desk will still demand the excess deposit, so you need a credit card with enough limit.
- Fuel: the local firms use same-level-in, same-level-out or deposit schemes. Goldcar-type full-to-full rates come with refuelling penalties. Refuel close to TFS and keep the receipt.

### Gaps
- I found no hard data on how far ahead Christmas stock sells out at TFS.
- TopCar's and Record Go's terms were not checked.
- TripAdvisor Tenerife forum threads were seen only as snippets, not read in full.
